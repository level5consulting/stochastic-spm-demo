Make your own in three steps

1Start from an exampleDownload the deck, script and config for either demo, or just play with them as they are.
2Edit one fileChange names, numbers and story in demo-config.json. Or ask Claude: the repo includes a skill that asks four questions and builds it for you.
3Run one commandpython3 build_all.py rebuilds the demo, the deck and the script together.
